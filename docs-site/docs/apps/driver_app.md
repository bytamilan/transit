# Driver App

Mounted, always-on, near-zero-interaction terminal for in-cab transit operations.
Confirming a duty is the primary interaction a driver needs — location reporting,
trip/stop detection, safety interlock, and occupancy prompts run seamlessly
in the background for the rest of the shift.

## Cockpit Safety & In-Cab Features

- **Day / Night Cockpit Palette**: High-contrast phosphor display engineered to eliminate night-driving glare.
- **Safety Interlock**: Screen controls and non-essential interactions automatically lock out when moving above safety speed thresholds.
- **1-Tap Crowding & Incident Reporting**: Rapid single-tap buttons for passenger occupancy and route incidents.
- **Diagnostic Device Readiness**: Pre-shift verification of GPS Always permissions, battery optimization exemptions, and OEM background survival.
